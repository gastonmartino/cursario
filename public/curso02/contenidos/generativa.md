### Chapter 2 - Metaversed Nature

Pixel perfect wilderness

![Metaversed Nature: Haemolacria, Plastic Natianta, The Marshal's Queer Aigrette](/curso02/imagenes/editorial/cap02-metaversed-nature-1.webp) *Haemolacria · Plastic Natianta · The Marshal's Queer Aigrette*

![Metaversed Nature: The Three-Body Problem, Diaspores, The Battle](/curso02/imagenes/editorial/cap02-metaversed-nature-2.webp) *The Three-Body Problem · Diaspores · The Battle*

![Metaversed Nature: Fly in the Ointment, Neural Rhythm](/curso02/imagenes/editorial/cap02-metaversed-nature-3.webp) *Fly in the Ointment · Neural Rhythm*

[View collection](https://objkt.com/curations/objkt/metaversed-nature-2a0787a6)

- **Haemolacria** — Just as the last flash of green was fading, tears of the deepest red hit the ground.
- **Plastic Natianta** — Plasticized seabed weaves its way through the food chain, back to the surface.
- **The Marshal's Queer Aigrette** — A prouder silhouette of the wearer, extending beyond the battlefield.
- **The Three-Body Problem** — Controlling the uncontrollable to become more controllable.
- **Diaspores** — The diaspora commences right after the vibrant cry of the lichens.
- **The Battle** — The struggle only starts after the beast has been tamed.
- **Fly in the Ointment** — After the convenient discovery of the insect, no one questioned the alleged benefits of the ointment again.
- **Neural Rhythm** — The synchronized oscillations of the media platforms will tirelessly attempt to modulate attentions until all memories dance to the same drumbeat.
